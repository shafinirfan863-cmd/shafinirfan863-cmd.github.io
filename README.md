## 🚀 Concept & Unconventional Philosophy
> **"Why is this unconventional?"**  
> This portfolio completely discards standard website conventions—eliminating traditional headers, footers, and menu bars in favor of an interactive sci-fi command deck. Visitors do not navigate through flat link lists; instead, they engage physical modular reactor pods and biometric portals to traverse distinct digital sectors. Every sector is an explorable station linked directly to the operator's central identity core.

## 🛠️ How to Customize Your Personal Details
1. **Your Photo**:
   - Place your picture (JPG or PNG) inside the `images/` folder as `profile.jpg`.
   - Open `index.html` around line 31 and change `src="images/avatar-default.svg"` to `src="images/profile.jpg"`.
2. **Your Homework 7 Link**:
   - Open `index.html` around line 118.
   - Update `href="../Homework_7/"` to your exact hosted GitHub Pages link for Homework 7 (e.g., `https://yourusername.github.io/CS_CRP/Homework_7/`).
